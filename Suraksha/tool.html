02 / Know what to do

Quick guides for urgent moments

Short, practical reminders for common emergencies. Follow instructions from emergency professionals whenever available.

🔥

Fire & smoke

Prioritise getting out safely over saving belongings.

Open fire guide ↗
  • Raise the alarm and leave by the safest available exit.
  • Stay low if there is smoke; do not use lifts.
  • Close doors behind you if safe, and stay outside.
  • Call 112 or the local fire service from a safe place.
  • Do not re-enter until officials say it is safe.
🚑

Accident or injury

Make the scene safer and get professional help.

Open accident guide ↗
  • Check for danger before approaching.
  • Call 112 and explain the location and what happened.
  • Do not move someone with a suspected neck or back injury unless there is immediate danger.
  • For severe bleeding, apply firm direct pressure with clean cloth if possible.
  • Follow the emergency operator’s instructions.
🌊

Floods & severe weather

Move away from rising water and follow official alerts.

Open weather guide ↗
  • Move to higher ground when flooding threatens.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Keep away from fallen power lines.
  • Follow official evacuation and weather advisories.
  • Keep your phone charged and carry essential medicines if evacuating.
🫁

Medical emergency

Get help early and follow trained guidance.

Open medical guide ↗
  • Call 112 or an ambulance service and describe symptoms clearly.
  • Check whether the person responds and is breathing normally.
  • If they are unresponsive and not breathing normally, follow dispatcher instructions for CPR.
  • Do not give food or drink to someone who is unconscious.
  • Stay with the person if it is safe to do so.
🧭

Lost or unsafe

Move towards a safer, populated place if possible.

Open personal safety guide ↗
  • Move to a well-lit public place or staffed location if safe.
  • Call a trusted person and clearly state where you are.
  • Use your phone’s location-sharing feature only with someone you trust.
  • If threatened or in immediate danger, call 112.
  • Avoid confronting a threatening person if you can safely leave.
⚡

Electrical hazard

Keep away from live wires and wet electrical equipment.

Open electrical guide ↗
  • Do not touch a person who may still be in contact with electricity.
  • Switch off the power only if you can do so safely.
  • Keep others away and call emergency services.
  • Do not approach fallen power lines, even if they appear inactive.
  • Seek medical help after an electric shock.
03 / First response

In any emergency

1 Check for danger. Do not put yourself or others at further risk.
2 Call for help. In India, call 112 for an emergency and give a clear location.
3 Explain simply. Say what happened, how many people need help, and any immediate hazards.
4 Follow instructions. Stay on the line when asked and follow the operator’s directions.
Before you need it

Make a small readiness plan

✓ Keep emergency contacts saved and accessible.
✓ Know two exits from your home, school, or workplace.
✓ Keep essential medicines, a torch, and a power bank where practical.
✓ Share your plan with someone you trust and review it occasionally.
